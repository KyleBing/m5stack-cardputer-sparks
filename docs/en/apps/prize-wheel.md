# Prize Wheel

Open with main menu `g`, then Games `3`.

A colorful segment wheel. Hold Space to charge a spin, or shake for medium power; when it stops, the fixed top pointer marks the winner and a gold banner shows `#N`.

## Shortcuts

| Key | Action |
|-----|--------|
| Hold `Space` / `GO` | Charge, then spin on release (full power at 1.8 s) |
| Shake device | Spin at about 55% power (rising-edge trigger) |
| `-` `=` | Item count − / + (2–12; works while spinning and resets) |
| `,` `.` | Same as `-` / `=` |
| `h` | Help |

## Notes

- While `Space` or the side `GO` button is held, a bottom `POWER` bar shows the live 0–100% charge.
- The pointer stays at twelve o'clock; the wheel spins and decelerates beneath it.
- After stopping, the winning segment is highlighted and the result banner slides in with a glint pass.
- Changing the segment count immediately stops the wheel and resets pose.
